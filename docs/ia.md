# Artificial Intelligence Usage Disclosure

During the development of **KubeLab — Group 5**, **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** was used as a technical and documentation support tool.

## Purpose of AI assistance

- Explaining concepts related to Proxmox, K3s, Kubernetes, MetalLB, Traefik, Helm, NFS, Argo CD, Prometheus, Loki, Grafana, Tailscale, and Doppler.
- Helping structure diagnostic commands, identify potential error causes, and interpret terminal output.
- Reviewing and drafting YAML manifest examples and verification scripts, subject to execution and validation by the project team.
- Preparing, refining, and organizing README files, technical guides, and evidence descriptions.

## Team responsibility

Implementation decisions, account and system access, command execution, hardware management, and functional verification remain the responsibility of the group members. AI-generated suggestions were compared with actual cluster and repository results before being adopted. Every team member must be able to explain the repository files and architecture during the live evaluation.

Private GitHub, Argo CD, PostgreSQL, Doppler, and Tailscale credentials must not appear in conversations, commits, or evidence. AI assistance does not replace testing or direct validation of the laboratory environment.
